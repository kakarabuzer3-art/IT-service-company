Privacy Policy

Last updated · 12 January 2026 · DPO: privacy@caravel.tech

1. Data we collect2. How we use it3. Legal bases4. Sharing5. Retention6. Your rights7. Security8. Cookies

1. Data we collect

We collect only what a professional relationship requires: contact details you provide, engagement records, and the minimum technical telemetry needed to operate our services securely.

We do not collect special-category data about visitors, and we never purchase third-party contact lists.

2. How we use it

Your data is used to deliver contracted services, respond to enquiries, meet legal obligations, and — with consent — send our monthly engineering logbook. No advertising profiles, ever.

3. Legal bases

Under GDPR we rely on contract performance (service delivery), legitimate interest (security and abuse prevention), consent (marketing), and legal obligation (tax and audit records).

4. Sharing

We share data only with sub-processors essential to delivery (cloud infrastructure, email delivery), each bound by DPAs. A current sub-processor list is available on request.

5. Retention

Enquiry data is deleted after 24 months of inactivity; contract records are retained for the statutory period (10 years) and then erased within 90 days.

6. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction and objection — exercisable by emailing privacy@caravel.tech. We respond within 30 days, usually within three.

7. Security

ISO 27001-certified controls, encryption in transit and at rest, annual third-party penetration tests, and a public responsible-disclosure program.

8. Cookies

This site uses one functional cookie for theme preference and privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics. No cross-site advertising cookies.
